# V33 Advanced Separation Lab

Added a named-spot separation diagnostics layer:
- Spot color library with save/load JSON.
- Multi-spot channel masks from sRGB/D65 LAB Delta-E76 matching.
- Channel coverage diagnostics.
- Pairwise overlap analysis.
- Priority/order knockout preview.
- Simple expand/minify trapping preview.
- Channel contact sheet export.
- Detailed separation report JSON.

Limitations: these are engineering/preview calculations. They are not calibrated ICC transforms, spectrophotometer profiles, RIP output, or press-validated trapping.
