# V34 Roadmap — Halftone & Film Lab

Added a channel-wise halftone/film engineering lab.

- LPI ↔ output-DPI cell-size calculation
- Rotated-cell halftone preview
- Circle/square/diamond/line dot shapes
- Dot density and film-density controls
- Per-channel PNG film export with DPI metadata
- Contact sheet and JSON manifest/report
- Reuses V33 named spot channel generation

Important: this is an engineering preview/export generator, not a calibrated RIP screen. Actual dot gain, mesh, exposure, ink deposit, film density and press behavior require production calibration.
