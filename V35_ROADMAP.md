# V35 Roadmap — Underbase & Dark-Garment Lab

Implemented:
- Adaptive, solid and luma underbase masks
- Strength and threshold controls
- Pixel-domain choke/spread
- Highlight protection and smoothing
- Dark garment proof previews
- Underbase coverage diagnostics
- Compare adaptive/solid/luma modes
- PNG mask + garment proof export
- JSON manifest/report

Limitations:
- Engineering preview/mask generator, not a calibrated RIP.
- Choke/spread are pixel-domain operations.
- Garment proof is visual and does not model real ink opacity, mesh, exposure, dot gain or fabric.
