# V47 Roadmap

- Print-shop operations layer linked to V42-V46 local job database.
- Per-job garment size quantities (S–5XL) and total quantity.
- Ink/material notes and material usage records.
- Operator assignment and machine/shift metadata.
- Production checklist with persistent completion state.
- Delivery status, tracking, recipient and delivery date.
- Final production report JSON combining job, quantities, checklist, materials, operators and delivery.
- Finalize operation marks delivery Ready and records an event.
- Local SQLite only; no claim of ERP/accounting integration.
